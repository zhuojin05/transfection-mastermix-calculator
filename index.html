🧪 Transfection Master Mix & Dilution Guide

1. Row Stoichiometry Modes

  • Mock (Filler Only): 100% empty-vector filler to hit total tube DNA mass.
  • Single + Filler: Specify fixed ng/well or match the molar dose of a plasmid in a 1:1 co-expression control; remainder is topped up with filler.
  • Fixed Total Molar (A:B): Splits total tube DNA proportionally by molecular length so molar ratio rA : rB holds (mA = Mtotal × (rA·LA) / (rA·LA + rB·LB)).
  • Anchor Titration + Filler: Holds Plasmid A fixed, calculates Plasmid B by molar ratio, and fills remainder with filler DNA.
  • Direct Mass Entry: Manual per-well or per-tube ng input with optional automatic filler top-up.

2. Minimal-Waste Multi-Tier Auto Dilution & Buffer Compensation

  • Any pipetting volume below Min Pipette (V_min) triggers the per-plasmid shared-dilution solver, picking the fewest standard tiers (e.g., 1:10, 1:20, 1:50, 1:100, or 2-step serial 1:200–1:10000 capped at 100× per step).
  • If a plasmid requires both a lower and higher compatible tier (e.g., 1:10 and 1:200), the prep planner chains them directly to minimize stock and diluent waste.
  • With Buffer Compensation enabled, extra volume added by diluted stocks is subtracted from the complexation buffer so all master mix tubes maintain equal final concentration.

3. PowerPoint Table & .pptx Workflows

  • Click 📋 Copy Table and paste directly into Microsoft PowerPoint (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V) to create an editable native slide table preserving shading, borders, and superscript dilution marks (†, ‡, §).
  • Drag & drop an existing well-map .pptx anywhere on the page (or click 📥 Import .pptx) to parse 6–96 well layouts, construct legends, and stoichiometries offline.
